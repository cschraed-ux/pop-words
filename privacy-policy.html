Pop Words

Privacy Policy · Effective October 1, 2026

The short version

Pop Words is made by PSU Pro, LLC. We built it to be a simple, friendly game that does not collect your information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, scores, statistics, daily puzzle history and any player names you type for party games are saved only on your device.

We never receive this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Pop Words Premium is a one-time purchase. The payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

To check whether Premium is unlocked, and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat identifies your purchase using an anonymous, app-specific identifier created by the app, together with the purchase receipt. It is not linked to your name, email address or any account, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf; see RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not run ads. We do not use analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal information. We do not ask you to create an account. The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

Pop Words is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing about you is sent to us, there is nothing for a parent to review or remove on our side.

Deleting your data

You are in control. To clear your scores, statistics and daily history, open Settings and choose “Reset stats & daily history”. To remove everything the app has saved, delete the app from your device.

Your Premium purchase belongs to your Apple or Google account, so it can be restored if you reinstall.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Please check back from time to time.
